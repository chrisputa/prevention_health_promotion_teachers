**Teacher health** (German: *Lehrergesundheit*) is the physical, mental and social health of teachers and the conditions at school that shape it. It is an emerging research field that studies the wellbeing of teachers and students together, because both determine how well a class can learn.

## Aims and scope

The course introduces future teachers to what is known about the health of their profession, how reliable that knowledge is, and what can be done about it. It is part of the lecture *Gesundheitsförderung und Prävention* for B.Sc. Performance & Health and teacher training students at Friedrich-Schiller-University Jena.

The scope runs in four steps: from the epidemiology of illness and sick leave among teachers and the limits of the available data, through prevention at the level of the school and the individual, to sleep as the most affordable stress prevention and, finally, social health, loneliness and a short conversation technique for difficult moments.

Throughout, the course separates what the data show from what we recommend, states the level of evidence for each measure, and follows one guiding principle: *structural prevention before behavioural prevention* (Verhältnisse vor Verhalten).

## Language

The **framework** of this course is written in **English**, while the **lectures** themselves are available in **German**.
